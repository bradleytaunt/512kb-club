How do I join The 512KB Club?

If you're interested in getting your site added to The 512KB Club, all you need to do is follow these instructions:

  1. Do a Debugbear Page Weight Scan on your website.
  2. Once complete, check the total "Page Weight" and make sure the uncompressed size of your site is less than 512KB.
  3. If your site satisfies this requirement, add it to the existing _data/sites.yml file (following the same format as other members) and submit a patch.
  4. I will then review your patch and merge it into master. Once merged, your site will be added to the list.

Note: I reserve the right to not add sites based on whether I think they're suitable to be added or not.
